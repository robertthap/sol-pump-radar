# Legal

This tool is provided as-is for personal research and personal trading. **Not financial advice.**

## Personal use only

- Do not accept other people's funds.
- Do not run this as a service.
- Do not market this as a signal provider.
- Do not share alerts in a way that could be construed as investment advice.

## Allowed

- Reacting to public on-chain data (events, holders, prices).
- Trading your own wallet with your own bot.
- Reading public APIs within their terms of service.

## Not allowed

- Wash trading, spoofing, market manipulation.
- Coordinated pump-and-dump schemes.
- Front-running other users' mempool intents.
- Using non-public insider information.

## Taxes

You are responsible for tracking and reporting your trades in your jurisdiction. The system writes a tax CSV export (Phase 12+). Keep your own records.

## Jurisdictional notes

Regulation of crypto trading varies by country. Confirm locally that:

1. Operating an automated trading bot on your own funds is permitted.
2. Crypto-to-fiat off-ramping is allowed and what tax regime applies.
3. There are no specific restrictions on memecoin trading.

When in doubt, consult a local lawyer / accountant.
