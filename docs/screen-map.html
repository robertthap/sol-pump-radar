AppChrome checks the path. / renders bare. Everything else goes into PortalLayout: the banner, then a sticky 56px nav, then a padded <main>. Each page centres its own content at up to 1200px (.app-page).

ModeBannercomponents/runtime/ModeBanner.tsx
Full-width strip above the nav. Green for demo, amber for a real wallet, red "REAL WALLET - LIVE TRADING" once live execution is on without dry run, grey when no wallet is picked. Adds "Trading paused" when the breaker has halted. It makes no requests of its own.
TopNavcomponents/layout/TopNav.tsx
Logo (links to Trade), Trade and Wallet tabs, then the bot's state, the Demo/Real pill, the glossary, pause/resume, Reset (demo only) and Log off. Below md, the glossary, halt and session buttons move into a ☰ drawer.
HaltButtoncomponents/HaltButton.tsx
"Pause trading" and "Resume trading". Each queues a halt or resume command and then reloads the page.
ModeGlossarycomponents/ModeGlossary.tsx
A "?" that opens a 288px popover explaining Demo vs Real. Closes on an outside click or Esc.

TickerProvidercomponents/ticker/TickerProvider.tsx
Not visible. Polls /api/ticker once and feeds all four regions. It also holds the chart target and the 10-minute sparkline history.
PnlHerocomponents/ticker/PnlHero.tsx · PnlSparkline.tsx
Left side: total P&L (realized + unrealized), its A$ value, Open and Realized, then the Invested, Value and Available tiles. Right side: the sparkline for the whole portfolio or one chosen position. The top edge is green or red with the P&L. When the data is STALE or errors, a Retry button and an error strip appear.
PositionListcomponents/ticker/PositionList.tsx
Open and Closed tabs with counts, and Sell all on the Open tab. The list scrolls after about 4½ rows (max 19rem). Closed rows show the exit reason, hold time and a DexScreener link.
PositionRowcomponents/ticker/PositionRow.tsx
Clicking the row expands it: prices, market caps, size, P&L, age, TP/SL, max hold and mint, plus "Show on chart" and "Open in DexScreener". Sell sits outside the expand button.
BotControlscomponents/ticker/BotControls.tsx
Strategy is always editable. When stopped, it shows the per-trade size, daily loss cap, max open positions and risk preset, plus Start bot. While running, those limits are read-only, joined by the exit rules, a slots bar and Stop bot. Flip "Bot running" in the bar above to see it.
LiveLogcomponents/ticker/LiveLog.tsx
Spans the full width under the grid. Shows OPENED, CLOSED, PARTIAL PROFIT, BOT, HALT and ERROR events only. Scrolls after 18rem.

overlaysModals, popovers, loading

These sit on top of the pages above. ModeGate is mounted once for the whole app in app/providers.tsx. The others belong to the component that opens them.

treeHow a page is assembled

The render path from the root layout to each page's regions.

app/layout.tsx                 RootLayout  <html><body>
└─ app/providers.tsx          AppProviders
   └─ PhantomConnectRoot       only when Phantom connect is enabled
      └─ TradingModeProvider   mode · session · demo balance · wallet flags
         ├─ RuntimePerfBeacon
         ├─ ChunkLoadRecovery
         ├─ ModeGate            overlay on /trade and /wallet when no session
         └─ AppChrome           pathname === "/" ?
            ├─ /        → app/page.tsx → LandingPage
            │                              └─ RealModeGate
            └─ else     → PortalLayout
                           ├─ ModeBanner
                           ├─ TopNav  (ModeGlossary, HaltButton)
                           └─ <main>
                              ├─ /trade  → TickerProvider
                              │             ├─ PnlHero → PnlSparkline
                              │             ├─ grid: PositionList → PositionRow · BotControls
                              │             └─ LiveLog
                              └─ /wallet → WalletPage
                                            ├─ SessionWalletBalance → WalletPanel → WalletSetupModal
                                            ├─ Status
                                            └─ WatchlistPanel
